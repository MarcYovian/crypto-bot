# BUSINESS.md: SMC QuantEngine

## Executive Summary

SMC QuantEngine is a production-grade, fully automated algorithmic trading platform designed to execute Smart Money Concepts (SMC) strategies on Binance USD-M Futures with institutional-quality risk management and operational transparency. The business model centers on a high-performance, asynchronous backend that autonomously identifies trade setups, executes orders with precision, and provides real-time monitoring through a FastAPI dashboard and Telegram interface.

This document outlines the core business logic, trading mechanics, risk framework, revenue model, and operational workflows that define how the system creates value for quantitative traders and manages financial risk.

## Trading Strategy & Execution Model

### Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Framework

The SMC strategy is built on the premise that institutional traders ("smart money") leave identifiable traces in price action. The bot identifies and trades these patterns with precision.

#### Pattern Recognition: Fair Value Gap (FVG) & Order Block (OB)

**Fair Value Gap (FVG)**
- A FVG occurs when three consecutive candles create a gap in price that has not yet been "filled" (revisited).
- **Bullish FVG**: Low of candle 2 > High of candle 1 (upward gap).
- **Bearish FVG**: High of candle 2 < Low of candle 1 (downward gap).
- The bot scans the last 3–4 closed 15-minute candles to identify unmitigated FVGs.

**Order Block (OB)**
- The OB is the last candle *before* the impulse move that created the FVG.
- It represents the zone where institutional buyers/sellers likely placed their orders.
- The bot uses the OB's high/low as the reference for entry and stop-loss placement.

**Mitigation Check**
- A FVG is "mitigated" if price has already retraced into the gap zone.
- The bot only trades unmitigated FVGs, ensuring the setup is still valid.

#### Higher Timeframe (HTF) Bias

To filter false signals and align with institutional bias:
- **Bullish Bias (BUY signals allowed)**: 4-hour candle close > 50-period EMA on the 4H timeframe.
- **Bearish Bias (SELL signals allowed)**: 4-hour candle close < 50-period EMA on the 4H timeframe.
- This ensures the bot only trades in the direction of the larger trend, reducing whipsaw losses.

#### Entry, Stop Loss, and Take Profit Mechanics

| Component | Logic | Rationale |
|:----------|:------|:----------|
| **Entry** | LIMIT order placed at the proximal edge of the OB (high for shorts, low for longs). | Ensures precise entry at institutional support/resistance. |
| **Stop Loss (SL)** | STOP_MARKET order placed slightly beyond the OB (with slippage buffer). | Protects against false breakouts; buffer accounts for execution slippage. |
| **Take Profit (TP)** | TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET order targeting 1:2 or 1:3 Risk/Reward ratio. | Locks in profits at predetermined levels; higher ratios reward larger moves. |

**Risk/Reward Calculation**
```
Risk (R) = Entry Price - Stop Loss Price
Reward (1R) = Entry Price + (Risk × Ratio)
Example: Entry 100, SL 98, Ratio 2:1 → TP = 100 + (2 × 2) = 104
```

## Risk Management Framework

### Position Sizing & Lot Calculation

The system enforces a strict **2% maximum risk per trade** of total account equity. Position size is calculated dynamically based on:

1. **Account Equity**: Current total balance (including unrealized PnL).
2. **Risk Percentage**: Fixed at 2% per trade.
3. **Entry-to-SL Distance**: The absolute price difference between entry and stop loss.
4. **Binance Symbol Constraints**: `minNotional`, `stepSize`, `tickSize`, `maxQty`.

**Calculation Formula**
```
Max Risk Amount = Account Equity × 0.02
Position Size (contracts) = Max Risk Amount / (Entry Price - SL Price)
Adjusted Position Size = Round(Position Size, stepSize)
Notional Value = Adjusted Position Size × Entry Price

Validation:
  IF Notional Value < minNotional OR Adjusted Position Size > maxQty:
    REJECT trade and log alert
  ELSE:
    EXECUTE trade
```

**Example**
```
Account Equity: $10,000
Max Risk: $10,000 × 0.02 = $200
Entry: 100 USDT, SL: 98 USDT
Risk per contract: 100 - 98 = 2 USDT
Position Size: 200 / 2 = 100 contracts
Binance minNotional: 5 USDT → 100 × 100 = 10,000 USDT ✓ (Valid)
```

### Slippage & Execution Safeguards

**Limit Order Strategy**
- Entry orders are placed as LIMIT orders at the OB edge to control execution price.
- If the order does not fill within a configurable timeout (e.g., 5 minutes), it is cancelled and the signal is re-evaluated.

**Market Order Fallback**
- If a LIMIT entry order expires, the bot may optionally place a MARKET order to capture the move.
- Upon market order execution, the actual fill price is retrieved from Binance and the position size is recalculated to maintain the 2% risk constraint.

**Stop Loss & Take Profit Execution**
- SL and TP orders are placed as STOP_MARKET and TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET orders respectively.
- These orders are triggered automatically by Binance when price reaches the specified level, ensuring execution even if the bot is temporarily offline.

### Drawdown & Account Protection

**Maximum Drawdown Limit**
- The system tracks cumulative daily losses. If daily losses exceed 5% of account equity, all scanning and trading is paused.
- A high-priority Telegram alert is sent to the operator.
- Trading resumes the following day or upon manual override.

**Consecutive Loss Limiter** (Future Feature)
- After 3 consecutive losing trades, the risk percentage is reduced to 1% for the next 5 trades.
- This dynamic scaling prevents emotional escalation and protects capital during drawdown periods.

### Failsafe & Circuit Breaker

**Critical Error Detection**
- Repeated authentication failures (> 5 consecutive failures).
- Database connection loss (> 30 seconds).
- Unrecoverable CCXT API errors (e.g., `InvalidAddress`, `InsufficientBalance`).

**Failsafe Response**
1. All scanning and trade execution is immediately paused.
2. All open positions are flagged for manual review (not auto-liquidated).
3. A high-priority Telegram alert is dispatched with error details.
4. The operator must manually investigate and restart the system.

## Operational Workflows

### Trade Lifecycle

```
1. SCAN PHASE (Every 15 minutes)
   ├─ Fetch 4H OHLCV data → Calculate 50-EMA → Determine HTF bias
   ├─ Fetch 15m OHLCV data (last 4 candles) → Identify FVG & OB
   ├─ Check if FVG is unmitigated (price has not retraced into gap)
   └─ If valid setup detected → Proceed to SIGNAL PHASE

2. SIGNAL PHASE
   ├─ Validate HTF bias (4H close vs. 50-EMA)
   ├─ Calculate entry, SL, TP prices
   ├─ Calculate position size (2% risk rule)
   ├─ Validate against Binance symbol constraints
   └─ If all checks pass → Proceed to EXECUTION PHASE

3. EXECUTION PHASE
   ├─ Place LIMIT entry order at OB edge
   ├─ Place STOP_MARKET SL order
   ├─ Place TAKE_PROFIT_MARKET TP order
   ├─ Log trade to database (status: PENDING)
   └─ Send Telegram notification: "Trade Signal Detected"

4. MONITORING PHASE
   ├─ WebSocket listener receives order updates from Binance
   ├─ Update order status in database (FILLED, PARTIALLY_FILLED, CANCELLED)
   ├─ If entry FILLED → Send Telegram: "Entry Filled"
   ├─ If TP FILLED → Send Telegram: "Take Profit Hit" + PnL
   ├─ If SL FILLED → Send Telegram: "Stop Loss Hit" + PnL
   └─ Close trade record in database (status: CLOSED)

5. RECONCILIATION PHASE (Every 5 minutes)
   ├─ Query Binance REST API for all open positions
   ├─ Compare with database state
   ├─ If mismatch detected → Log alert and sync database
   └─ Ensure no orphaned orders or positions
```

### Watchlist Management

**Dynamic Watchlist Updates**
- The system maintains an active `watchlist` table in PostgreSQL.
- Symbols can be added or removed via the FastAPI `/v1/watchlist` endpoint without restarting the bot.
- The SMC Scanner iterates only through active symbols, avoiding unnecessary API calls and rate limit exhaustion.

**Watchlist Entry Schema**
```
watchlist:
  - symbol (e.g., "BTC/USDT")
  - is_active (boolean)
  - added_at (timestamp)
  - removed_at (nullable timestamp)
  - notes (optional trader notes)
```

### Manual Override & Emergency Controls

**Telegram Commands**
- `/close_all`: Immediately liquidates all open positions at market price. Used in emergency scenarios (e.g., critical news, system malfunction).
- `/status`: Returns current account equity, unrealized PnL, and list of open positions.
- `/pause`: Pauses scanning and trade execution without closing positions.
- `/resume`: Resumes scanning and trade execution.

**API Endpoints for Overrides**
- `POST /v1/positions/close-all`: Market-liquidates all positions (Admin only).
- `POST /v1/positions/{position_id}/close`: Closes a specific position (Admin only).
- `POST /v1/trading/pause`: Pauses the trading engine (Admin only).
- `POST /v1/trading/resume`: Resumes the trading engine (Admin only).

## Error Handling & Recovery Strategy

### CCXT API Errors

| Error Type | Cause | Recovery Strategy |
|:-----------|:------|:------------------|
| `RateLimitExceeded` | Too many API calls in short time. | Exponential backoff (1s → 2s → 4s → 8s); skip current scan cycle. |
| `NetworkError` | Temporary network connectivity issue. | Retry up to 3 times with 2-second intervals. |
| `InvalidAddress` | Malformed symbol or order parameters. | Log error; skip symbol; alert operator. |
| `InsufficientBalance` | Account has insufficient margin. | Reject trade; reduce position size; alert operator. |
| `OrderNotFound` | Order ID does not exist on exchange. | Reconcile via REST API; update database. |

### Database Errors

| Error Type | Cause | Recovery Strategy |
|:-----------|:------|:------------------|
| **Connection Loss** | PostgreSQL unavailable. | Retry connection every 5 seconds; pause trading after 30 seconds; alert operator. |
| **Transaction Timeout** | Query takes too long. | Rollback transaction; retry with shorter timeout. |
| **Constraint Violation** | Duplicate trade ID or invalid foreign key. | Log error; investigate data integrity; alert operator. |

### System-Level Failsafes

**Watchdog Timer**
- A background task checks the health of the SMC Scanner every 30 seconds.
- If no scan has completed in the last 60 seconds, the watchdog triggers a failsafe alert.

**Database Reconciliation**
- Every 5 minutes, the system queries Binance REST API for all open positions and orders.
- Compares with database state; if mismatch detected, syncs database and logs alert.

**Telegram Heartbeat**
- Every 6 hours, the bot sends a "System Healthy" message to the operator.
- If heartbeat fails, the operator is alerted to check system status.

## Compliance & Risk Disclosure

### Regulatory Considerations

- The system operates on Binance USD-M Futures, which are derivatives and carry significant risk.
- Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with local regulations regarding cryptocurrency trading and derivatives.
- The system does not provide financial advice; it is a tool for executing pre-defined trading strategies.

### Risk Warnings

- **Leverage Risk**: Futures trading involves leverage, which can amplify losses beyond the initial investment.
- **Slippage Risk**: Actual execution prices may differ from expected prices, especially during high volatility.
- **Liquidation Risk**: If account equity falls below maintenance margin, positions may be forcibly liquidated by Binance.
- **Technology Risk**: System failures, network outages, or bugs could result in unintended trades or losses.
- **Strategy Risk**: The SMC strategy, while systematic, is not guaranteed to be profitable. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

### Operator Responsibilities

- Monitor the system regularly (at least daily).
- Maintain adequate account balance to avoid liquidation.
- Keep API keys secure and rotate them periodically.
- Review trade logs and PnL reports weekly.
- Test strategy changes in Shadow Trading Mode before deploying to live trading.
